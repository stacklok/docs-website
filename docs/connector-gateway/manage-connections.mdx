A _connection_ links you to one connector. The Connector Gateway serves a
connector's tools to your MCP clients only after you connect to it, and you
decide which of its tools stay on. Your connections and tool choices apply to
every client that signs in to the gateway as you.

Manage your connections in the console's user experience. If you also have
administrator access, open the account menu and choose **User** under
**Experience**. Then select **Connectors** under **Gateways**.

## Connect to a connector

The **Tools** tab lists the connectors your administrator has granted to you.
Connectors you've connected to appear under **Enabled**, and the rest appear
under **Available**.

1. Open a connector under **Available** and select **Connect**.
2. If the connector's **Access** section shows **Company SSO required**,
   complete the sign-in in the window that opens. The gateway stores the
   resulting credential and uses it for your calls to that connector.

Connectors that need no sign-in, shown as **No authentication required** or
**Managed credential**, connect immediately.

The connector's status badge then shows **Connected**, and its tools appear in
your MCP clients the next time they list tools.

## Choose which tools your clients see

A connector's detail page lists its tools in a **Tools** section, grouped into
**Destructive tools**, **Read-only tools**, and **Other tools**. Turn off any
tool you don't want your clients to call, or use **Disable all** on a group.

Your choices narrow the tools your administrator grants you. The gateway hides
the tools you turned off from your clients and rejects calls to them.

## Pause or disconnect

The **Connection** card on a connector's detail page has two ways to stop using
a connector:

- **Pause** hides the connector's tools from your clients and keeps your tool
  choices. The badge shows **Paused**, and **Resume** restores the connector
  with the same tools. You can edit tool choices while paused; they apply when
  you resume.
- **Disconnect** removes the connection and your tool choices, and moves the
  connector back to **Available**.

## Reconnect when sign-in expires

When the credential for a **Company SSO required** connector expires or is no
longer usable, the badge shows **Reconnect required** and the connector's tools
stop working. Select **Reconnect** and complete the sign-in again.

An administrator change to the connector's identity provider, such as new scopes
or a new client ID, also requires you to reconnect. See
[How users authorize connectors](./connector-authentication.mdx#how-users-authorize-connectors).

## When changes take effect

The gateway reads your connections on every request, so connecting, pausing, and
tool changes apply to your next tool list or tool call. Most MCP clients keep
the tool list from when they connected. If a client still shows old tools,
refresh its tool list or restart it.

## Review your usage

The **Usage** tab summarizes your own tool calls for the last 24 hours, 7 days,
or 30 days, broken down by connector. It stays empty until your administrator
turns on tool call recording. See
[Record tool calls](./tool-usage.mdx#record-tool-calls).

## Manage connections through the API

The Enterprise Manager exposes the same operations under `/v1/me/connections`
for scripts and custom tooling. Every route acts on the connections of the user
who owns the access token, an Enterprise Manager token from your corporate
identity provider.

| Operation                    | Route                                     |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| List your connections        | `GET /v1/me/connections`                  |
| Connect to a connector       | `POST /v1/me/connections`                 |
| Pause or resume a connection | `PUT /v1/me/connections/{id}/enabled`     |
| Replace the tool selection   | `PUT /v1/me/connections/{id}/tool-policy` |
| Disconnect                   | `DELETE /v1/me/connections/{id}`          |

A tool selection has two fields. With `tools_enabled_by_default: true`, every
tool is on except those listed in `tools_exception_list`. With `false`, only the
listed tools are on. The list takes up to 200 of the backend's own tool names,
such as `ask_question`, without the connector prefix your client shows, such as
`DeepWiki_<ID>_ask_question`.

```bash
curl -X PUT \
  "https://<ENTERPRISE_MANAGER_HOST>/v1/me/connections/<CONNECTION_ID>/tool-policy" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer <ACCESS_TOKEN>" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"tools_enabled_by_default": false, "tools_exception_list": ["search_issues", "get_issue"]}'
```

See the
[Enterprise Manager API reference](../platform/enterprise-platform/api-reference.mdx#enterprise-manager)
for request and response details.

## Troubleshooting

<details>
<summary>A connector you expect isn't listed</summary>

The **Tools** tab shows only connectors your administrator granted to a group
you belong to, and only while the connector is in service. If you see "No
connectors available to you yet", ask your administrator to check the
connector's [connector policy](./connector-policies.mdx).

</details>
