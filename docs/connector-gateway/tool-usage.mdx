The **Tool usage** screen summarizes calls through the Connector Gateway.

## What it shows

Select a time range to view **Tool calls**, **Active connectors**, **Top
connector**, and the tool call trend.

Use **By connector** to compare traffic across integrations. Use **By tool** to
identify the capabilities receiving traffic.

The screen reports tool calls only. Review model traffic in the AI Gateway.

If a registered connector does not appear, check its state and group grants on
the [Connectors](./connectors.mdx) screen. Then confirm that clients can reach
the Connector Gateway.
