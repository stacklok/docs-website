The administration experience is organized into three groups, matching the three
questions an administrator usually arrives with.

**Cost management** answers what the organization is spending and what it is
allowed to spend: [Organization](./organization-spend.mdx) for the spend
picture, [Budgets](./budgets.mdx) for the caps.

**Connectors** answers which tools exist and who may use them:
[Connectors](./connectors.mdx) to register them and grant access,
[Tool usage](./tool-usage.mdx) to see what is actually being called. The
identity providers and managed secrets in this group are documented with the
rest of the directory, in
[Identity providers](../../enterprise-directory/identity-providers.mdx) and
[Managed secrets](../../enterprise-directory/managed-secrets.mdx).

**Platform** answers who your people are. See
[Users and groups](../../enterprise-directory/users-and-groups.mdx).

## Getting administrative access

Administration requires the platform admin grant. It is one shared grant rather
than a role per feature, so the same grant covers budgets, connectors, and
directory administration.
