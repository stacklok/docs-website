The **Organization** screen is the spend overview: what the organization has
spent on model calls, how that compares with the preceding period, and which
groups and users are approaching or exceeding their limits.

## Reading the summary

The top of the screen shows total spend for the last 30 days, updated daily,
with a chart of the daily trend. Alongside it is a short-term comparison: spend
over the last seven days against the seven before it, with the percentage change.

Those two windows answer different questions. The 30-day total is what you report;
the 7-day comparison is what tells you something changed recently, which is the
more useful of the two for catching a runaway agent or a newly onboarded team.

## Usage by group and by user

Below the chart, a table breaks spend down, with tabs for groups and for users.
Each row shows spend, the applicable budget, and utilization as a percentage.

Utilization is the column to sort on. A group at 101% has already had requests
refused, since [budget enforcement](../../enterprise-ai-gateway/budgets-and-pricing.mdx)
denies rather than warns. Filters for under 80%, 80 to 100%, and over 100% are
there to make that triage quick.

A group with spend but no budget is worth attention too: it means traffic is
being attributed but nothing is capping it.

## What the numbers are

Spend is computed from each provider's reported token usage, priced against the
active pricing catalog. So a figure here is only as accurate as the rates you have
published, and a model with no rate does not appear as free traffic, it appears as
refused requests. See
[Budgets and pricing](../../enterprise-ai-gateway/budgets-and-pricing.mdx).

Group figures follow inherited membership, so a user in a subgroup contributes to
the parent group's total.
