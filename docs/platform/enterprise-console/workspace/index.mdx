Your workspace is the part of the console about your own work rather than the
organization's. It answers four questions: where do I point my tools, what can I
reach, what have I used, and how do I authenticate a script.

## The home screen

Signing in lands you on a home screen with three things.

**Your gateway endpoints**, for the AI gateway and the MCP gateway, each with a
health indicator and a copy button. These are the addresses your tools need, and
copying them from here is more reliable than typing them from a document,
because they are this deployment's real addresses.

**Usage today**, showing what you have spent against your daily budget, with the
remainder. This is your own figure, not the organization's.

**Top connectors**, the tools you have been calling most. Empty until you have
made tool calls through a connector.

## Getting set up

If you are new, go to [Connect a client](./connect-a-client.mdx). It covers both
gateways and points at the in-product setup instructions, which are generated
with your deployment's real endpoints already filled in.
