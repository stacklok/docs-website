The **Tool usage** screen shows the tool calls your own clients have made through
the MCP gateway: how many, through which connectors, and for which tools.

Unlike [model usage](./model-usage.mdx), there is no budget attached. Tool calls
are counted for visibility rather than capped, so this screen is about
understanding your own activity rather than staying within a limit.

## What it is for

Two practical uses.

**Checking that a connector works.** After enabling a connector and pointing a
client at it, a call appearing here is the confirmation that the whole path is
functioning. Nothing appearing means the client is not actually reaching the
gateway, which is usually a configuration problem in the client.

**Trimming what you have enabled.** A connector you enabled and never call is
adding tools to your client's choices for no benefit. See
[Your connectors](./connectors.mdx) for why that matters.
