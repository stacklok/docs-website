### Summary of changes

- Added `thv secret upgrade-protection` guidance in `docs/toolhive/guides-cli/secrets-management.mdx`, explaining when to migrate legacy encrypted stores to Argon2id and the one-way compatibility break.
- Updated `docs/toolhive/guides-vmcp/tool-aggregation.mdx` to note that the `priority` strategy now drops every candidate in a tool-name collision when any conflicting backend is absent from `priorityOrder` (verified against `pkg/vmcp/aggregator/priority_resolver.go`).
- Updated `docs/toolhive/guides-k8s/embedded-auth-server-k8s.mdx` and `docs/toolhive/guides-vmcp/embedded-auth-server-vmcp.mdx` to require each upstream provider's `redirectUri` to share the browser-facing authorize hostname (`issuer` or `authorizationEndpointBaseUrl`), because `/oauth/callback` binds each login to the browser that started it with a host-only cookie.
